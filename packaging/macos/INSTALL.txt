Encre for macOS

FIRST LAUNCH

Encre is not notarised, so macOS blocks the first launch with a message
about an unidentified developer.

Method 1
  1. Right-click (or Control-click) Encre.app
  2. Choose "Open"
  3. Click "Open" in the dialog

Method 2
  1. Try to open Encre normally; it is blocked
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security
  3. Next to "Encre was blocked", click "Open Anyway"

Method 3 (Terminal)
  xattr -cr /path/to/Encre.app

AFTER THAT
  - Encre lives in the menu bar; there is no Dock icon
  - Grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring when prompted, or hotkeys stay silent
  - Later launches open normally
